## 1.1 The coverage transition **[Q2, ONE RULE FOR M5-M8]**

Every coverage predicate in Step 1 named a population and never said what
satisfies completeness. One rule now governs all of them, instantiated at each
site by naming its population `U`:

```
COVERAGE(U)

COMPLETE     the population bound U is established;
             the declared construction or search procedure has been executed over
             that bound;
             every result-relevant member of U is accounted for;
             and no live bounded unexamined source can add another result-relevant
             member

INCOMPLETE   affirmative evidence identifies at least one result-relevant member
             of the established bounded population U that is not accounted for

UNRESOLVED   otherwise, including
               the population bound is not established, or
               a bounded unexamined source could contain a result-relevant member
               but no omitted member is affirmatively established
```

> **An unexamined region is not `INCOMPLETE`.** That would convert missing evidence
> into an affirmative omission, which is R4 at the coverage layer.

B3 §5.2's attachment-locus coverage is the **precedent** this generalises, not a
special case. The four populations:

```
M5  BENEFICIARY COVERAGE      U = bounded principals for whom acceptance of F
                                  could advance ρ
M6  BASIS ENUMERATION         U = inclusion-minimal sufficient bases within
                                  E(F, ρ), per §3.3.1
M7  DEPENDENCY VECTOR         U = applicable D30 vector instances generated from
                                  (vector type x evidence component or source
                                   x B x W x F x ρ x interval and context)
M8  CLASS REFINEMENT          U = bounded result-relevant class-version candidates
                                  generated by the declared refinement and
                                  challenge procedure within the discovery scope
```

Two consequences worth stating because they were not obvious to me:

**M7 coverage may be `COMPLETE` while individual vectors are `UNRESOLVED`.**
Population completeness and member-state resolution are separate questions, and
conflating them would have made vector coverage unreachable whenever any single
vector was unresolved.

**M8 does not mean every imaginable class.** It means every candidate inside a
positively bounded refinement and discovery universe, so bare hypothetical
refinements do not block closure — the same rule D14.1 and D15.1 apply to
relations and loci.

## 2. Trust root **[PER ANALYSIS, IN IDENTITY]**

> A **trust-root declaration `T`** names the parties, registries, or attestation
> mechanisms whose records are **eligible for root-base treatment subject to
> §3.4.1**.

The earlier wording said their records are *taken as independent without further
test*, which asserted the output §3.4.1 now derives under conditions. `T` is
> **versioned and per analysis**. An artifact may carry several analyses under
> different roots; one analysis has one `T`.

> **Support is written `CORROBORATED[T]`.** `CORROBORATED[T₁]` and
> `CORROBORATED[T₂]` are not interchangeable. Changing `T` produces a **new
> analysis**, as changing a consequence selection or a versioned overlay input
> does, and D24 sensitivity records any structural result that changes under an
> alternative declared root.

> **`T` identifies the root-base regime. The declaration does not itself terminate
> the regress.** Termination occurs only through §3.4.1 and §3.4.2, including the
> scoped assumptions `WF-T` requires.

`T` is **not** a proof of independence. The honest claim is *independent relative
to `T`*, and it now has an executable consequence rather than a disclaimer. The
prior wording said the declaration terminated the regress, which the repaired
machinery contradicts.

## 3. Beneficiary set **[PROPOSITION-RELATIVE]**

*Improve* introduced an ordering over structural results that MICRM refuses to
define elsewhere: whether established closure benefits a principal depends on what
it is being used to show.

> **`B(F, ρ)`** is every principal for whom acceptance of finding `F` would advance
> the **explicitly named result proposition `ρ`** — structural or
> analysis-structural — whose acceptance could affect the analysis.

`ρ` ranges over both, because D27 uses `ρ = class c admissible` and D29 uses
`ρ = the characterisation itself`, and neither is a MICRM structural output. The
prior wording said *structural proposition* and then used the word in two senses,
so discipline **M** did not type-check.

```
ρ examples   structural exclusion of P
             PATH NON-EXCLUDED for (P, c)
             attachment ABSENT for (P, c, A)
             closure ESTABLISHED for (P, τ)
```

Support is always recorded **with the proposition it was used for**. The same
evidence may be corroborated for one `ρ` and asserted for another, which the prior
definition could not express.

### 3.1 Beneficiary coverage **[NEW]**

`B(F, ρ)` is a universal population claim, and omitting a beneficiary deletes
every vector that depends on it: with `B` declared empty, unilateral control by
the actual beneficiary is never tested.

```
BENEFICIARY COVERAGE
    relation   COMPLETE / INCOMPLETE / UNRESOLVED
               the analysis accounts for every bounded principal for whom
               acceptance of F could advance **the named ρ**, structural or
               analysis-structural, per §1.1's U for M5
    support    CORROBORATED[T] / ASSERTED / UNRESOLVED
```

> **A finding cannot become `INDEPENDENT` while its beneficiary population is
> unestablished.** Incomplete or unsupported beneficiary coverage yields
> independence `UNRESOLVED`.

### 3.2 Support basis **[NEW]**

> **`W(F, ρ)`** is the **identified evidence set relied upon** to establish `F` for
> `ρ`. D30 evaluates the independence of a basis, **not of every record that
> mentions `F`**.

A finding may have several sufficient bases. Evaluating them as one pool let a
beneficiary-controlled record contaminate a finding that an independent sensor
already established, and would equally let an analyst quietly retain only the
favourable records.

### 3.3 Basis sufficiency **[NEW]**

`W` was introduced to stop the favourable-subset attack and did not close it:
omit a necessary dependent record, call the independent remainder *sufficient*,
and collect `CORROBORATED[T]`.

```
BASIS SUFFICIENCY(W, F, ρ)
    relation   SUFFICIENT     F follows from W under F's governing rule, by a
                              reproducible derivation
               INSUFFICIENT   it does not
               UNRESOLVED     the derivation is not established
    support    CORROBORATED[T] / ASSERTED / UNRESOLVED, where sufficiency requires
               evidentiary interpretation rather than a purely formal derivation
```

> **Only a basis that is `SUFFICIENT` with adequate support participates in the
> aggregation.** A declared basis whose sufficiency is `UNRESOLVED` contributes
> nothing in either direction.

### 3.3.1 Minimal sufficient bases **[M6 REPAIR]**

*Every sufficient basis* is not a well-founded population: if `{A, B}` is
sufficient then `{A, B, C}`, `{A, B, D}` and every other irrelevant superset is
too, and the population to cover explodes for bookkeeping reasons.

```
MINIMAL-SUFFICIENT-BASIS(W, F, ρ, E)
    W ⊆ E
    BASIS SUFFICIENCY(W, F, ρ) = SUFFICIENT
    and no proper subset W' ⊂ W has BASIS SUFFICIENCY(W', F, ρ) = SUFFICIENT

U_basis(F, ρ, E) = all inclusion-minimal sufficient bases within E
```

### 3.3.2 The evidence universe must itself be closed **[POPULATION-BOUND REPAIR]**

`U_basis` is defined relative to `E(F, ρ)`, so a merely **declared** `E` moves the
favourable-subset attack one level outward instead of stopping it:

```
real bounded evidence   {A, B}       A alone sufficient and independent
                                     B alone sufficient and dependent
analyst declares E      {A}
U_basis                 {{A}}
BASIS ENUMERATION COVERAGE over U_basis   COMPLETE
positive result obtained; B never enters the population
```

> **`E(F, ρ)` is an *established* bounded evidence universe, not a declared one.**
>
> **A claimant's declaration alone cannot close `E`.** Where the closure of `E`
> rests on the search record of a principal in `B(F, ρ)`, it is `ASSERTED` by
> construction, per §3.1's general form.

The outer universe is itself a coverage predicate, on the same three-state
discipline as every other. The prior generation gave it a **boundary requirement
and no state machine**: it said what defeats `COMPLETE` and never what state
follows, so an affirmatively identified omitted source outside `E` had no
derivable coverage state.

```
EVIDENCE UNIVERSE COVERAGE(F, ρ, E)

COMPLETE     the bounded result-relevant evidence-source population is
             established; every source is accounted for; and no bounded outside
             source can generate another sufficient basis

INCOMPLETE   affirmative evidence identifies an omitted bounded source that can
             generate another sufficient basis

UNRESOLVED   a bounded unexamined source could generate another sufficient basis,
             but no omitted sufficient source or basis is established

support      CORROBORATED[T] / ASSERTED / UNRESOLVED, per §3.1's general form
```

### 3.3.3 Outer-to-inner composition **[M12 REPAIR]**

The prior generation gave the outer predicate a state machine and left the
composition producing only *not `COMPLETE`* — **which is not one of the three
states**, so the inner relation remained underivable wherever the outer one was
not `COMPLETE`. It also gated the inner **relation** on the outer **support**
through an undefined phrase, *adequate support*, which puts support back inside a
relation rule.

**Relation, with support kept out of it entirely:**

```
BASIS ENUMERATION COVERAGE relation

if EVIDENCE UNIVERSE COVERAGE = INCOMPLETE   ->  INCOMPLETE
else if EVIDENCE UNIVERSE COVERAGE = UNRESOLVED ->  UNRESOLVED
else                                          ->  apply COVERAGE(U_basis) to the
                                                  inclusion-minimal bases within
                                                  that established E:
                                                    COMPLETE   -> COMPLETE
                                                    INCOMPLETE -> INCOMPLETE
                                                    UNRESOLVED -> UNRESOLVED
```

> **The outer relation decides whether the inner population is structurally
> evaluable. The outer support constrains the inner support, and never the inner
> relation.** The phrase *adequate support* is withdrawn.

**Support, as a conjunction rather than a special ordering rule:**

```
BASIS ENUMERATION COVERAGE is a conjunctive finding over two premises:
    the E-universe coverage premise
    the within-E basis-enumeration premise

Its identified support basis W must establish BOTH premises for the same
proposition, and D30 then determines CORROBORATED[T] / ASSERTED / UNRESOLVED in
the ordinary way.
```

No support-ordering rule is invented for M12: the conjunction is a finding like
any other, and §3.2, §3.3 and §9 govern its support unchanged.

> The R4 separation holds at this layer as at every other: **an unexamined bounded
> source is `UNRESOLVED`, and only an affirmatively identified omitted source is
> `INCOMPLETE`.** A source's capacity to hold a sufficient record is not evidence
> that it does.

**The general form of this defect**, recorded because the transition and
rule-conflict searches are both blind to it:

> Where a population is defined **relative to another declared universe**, that
> outer universe needs its own boundary rule. Naming the inner population is not
> enough if the outer one can be chosen.

`BASIS ENUMERATION COVERAGE` quantifies over `U_basis` within a closed `E`.

```
basis_identity =  F
                  ρ
                  evidence-universe version
                  governing derivation or rule version
                  canonical set of evidence-record identities in W
```

The **evidence-universe version** is in the identity because a newly discovered
record can change whether an existing basis is still minimal, and that must change
the basis's identity rather than silently alter it.

> **Dependency-equivalence classes are not the normative unit.** Collapsing bases
> because they currently produce the same D30 result is a reasonable reporting or
> computation optimisation, and as a normative unit it would hide the provenance
> distinction `W(F, ρ)` was introduced to preserve.

```
BASIS ENUMERATION COVERAGE(F, ρ)
    relation   COMPLETE / INCOMPLETE / UNRESOLVED
               every member of U_basis(F, ρ, E) — the inclusion-minimal
               sufficient bases of §3.3.1 — is accounted for
    support    CORROBORATED[T] / ASSERTED / UNRESOLVED
```

```
any participating basis INDEPENDENT    -> CORROBORATED[T]
                                          existential; no basis-set coverage needed

else any participating basis DEPENDENT -> ASSERTED, but only where BASIS
                                          ENUMERATION COVERAGE is COMPLETE +
                                          CORROBORATED[T]; otherwise UNRESOLVED

else                                   -> UNRESOLVED, and where basis-set coverage
                                          is not established this is the only
                                          available result
```

> The negative aggregations are **universal claims over the basis set** — *no
> independent basis exists*, *no participating basis exists* — and take the
> coverage discipline. The positive aggregation is existential and does not.

The basis that carried the status is recorded, so the provenance of a support
result is visible rather than inferred.

### 3.4 The trust-root base case **[C3 REPAIR — RATIONALE ONLY]**

Every meta-support finding demands `CORROBORATED[T]`, and nothing said how one is
ever obtained without invoking D30 again. That is what the base case exists to
solve.

> **Root neutrality is a condition on `T`, not a consequence of declaring it**, and
> **a declared trust root cannot license evidence controlled by the party it
> benefits.** Neutrality is per finding and per proposition, since a registry may
> be neutral for one `ρ` and beneficiary-controlled for another.

**This section is rationale. The operative definition of `ROOT-NEUTRAL` is §3.4.1
alone.** The prior generation left an older, narrower test here — no beneficiary
generation, retention, disclosure or destruction — beside the wider one below, so
a basis with beneficiary **selective-schema** control satisfied one definition and
failed the other. Two live definitions of one predicate, which is defect class 2.

### 3.4.1 The base-case output **[M2 REPAIR]**

The base case said what may terminate and never what it yields, while §9.3
consumed `INDEPENDENT(W)` and `CORROBORATED[T]` downstream.

```
a support basis W is ROOT-ADMITTED when every member is admitted directly by T

ROOT-ADMITTED(W) and ROOT-NEUTRAL(W)   ->  INDEPENDENT(W) by declaration
                                           -> CORROBORATED[T] where W is also
                                              SUFFICIENT per §3.3
otherwise                              ->  the ordinary §9.3 path applies
```

> **`INDEPENDENT` here is held by declaration, not established by test.** The
> honest reading is *independent relative to `T`*, and any result resting on it is
> reported as resting on the declared root, per §2.

**`ROOT-NEUTRAL` is widened to the full vector set**, which is the constraint that
made this repair worth waiting for. The prior neutrality test asked only about
generation, retention, disclosure and destruction:

```
ROOT-NEUTRAL(W) requires ALL of:

  BENEFICIARY COVERAGE(F, ρ)        COMPLETE + CORROBORATED[T]
  DEPENDENCY VECTOR COVERAGE(W)     COMPLETE + CORROBORATED[T]
  every covered vector instance     ABSENT + CORROBORATED[T] per §9.1.2

  over the vector set of §9.1:
    unilateral control        shared trust root       dependent capture
    selective schema control  colluding principals    recorder compromise
    forward-integrity failure clock control
```

**The coverage conjuncts are the repair.** Without them this was still executable:

```
beneficiary population incomplete
declared beneficiaries' vectors all ABSENT
W root-admitted
   §3.1   -> independence UNRESOLVED
   §3.4.1 -> INDEPENDENT
```

An omitted beneficiary or an unenumerated evidence source generates further vector
instances, so **naming vector types is not enough** — the populations themselves
must be covered.

### 3.4.2 Scoped assumptions, and why the base case needs them
**[M10 and CIRCULAR-1 REPAIR]**

As written, §3.4.1 was **not a base case**. Its prerequisites demand
`CORROBORATED[T]`, which demands `INDEPENDENT` support, which demands the same
coverage and vector machinery, which terminates only at a root-admitted basis:

```
ROOT-NEUTRAL(W)  ->  coverage + vectors at CORROBORATED[T]
CORROBORATED[T]  ->  INDEPENDENT support
INDEPENDENT      ->  the same coverage and vector machinery
termination      ->  a neutral root-admitted basis
```

The recursion returns to the result it exists to terminate, and §3.4's cycle rule
then sends it to `UNRESOLVED`. **A base case that cannot be reached is not a base
case.** The escape must be executable, and the prior text gave the assumption a
recording obligation and no effect.

> **A `T`-scoped assumption is a declared axiom that discharges named
> prerequisites without invoking any support derivation.**
>
> ```
> T-ASSUMPTION declares
>     populations closed    which of the beneficiary and vector populations are
>                           taken as covered for bases admitted by the named root
>     relations fixed       named vector INSTANCES, or named vector types
>                           universally over an explicitly closed vector-instance
>                           population for the named support node
>     bases in scope        the root-admitted bases the assumption covers
> ```
>
> **A type name alone is not a dependency-relation instance.** M9's unit is
> `dependency_vector(type, source, B, W, F, ρ, window[, context])`, and an
> assumption must reach that unit or close the population it ranges over.
>
> ```
> ROOT-NEUTRAL(W) holds when, for each prerequisite, either
>     the prerequisite is established by a support chain that itself terminates,
>     or a T-assumption in scope discharges it BY DECLARATION
> ```
>
> **Discharge by declaration invokes nothing**, which is what makes termination
> finite.

### 3.4.3 `WF-T-CONSISTENCY` **[NEW]**

The assumption as first written could discharge a prerequisite the evidence had
already decided the other way:

```
evidence establishes            selective-schema vector v = PRESENT
T-ASSUMPTION for the same basis vector population closed
                                selective-schema control taken as ABSENT
discharges the prerequisite  -> ROOT-NEUTRAL -> INDEPENDENT(W) by declaration
```

while ordinary D30 returns `DEPENDENT(W)` on the same facts. That contradicts the
principle stated immediately above the base case: **a declared root cannot license
evidence controlled by the party it benefits.**

> **A `T`-assumption may discharge uncertainty. It may not override an
> affirmatively established contrary relation.**
>
> ```
> population-closed assumption   cannot discharge a prerequisite where the
>                                corresponding coverage relation is INCOMPLETE
>
> relation-ABSENT assumption     cannot discharge a vector instance whose
>                                dependency relation is PRESENT
>
> conflict                       where an in-scope T-assumption conflicts with an
>                                affirmatively established contrary relation, that
>                                assumption is INVALID for the affected support
>                                node and cannot establish ROOT-NEUTRAL
>                                the ordinary §9.3 path remains available
> ```

**This is a well-formedness condition, not another support rule.** The assumption
is not weighed against the evidence; it is disqualified by it.

The general principle, and the sentence to carry forward:

> **`T` may close unresolvedness by declaration. It may not negate affirmative
> contrary evidence by declaration.**

Note that this bites in both directions of support: a `PRESENT` vector disqualifies
the assumption whether that vector is `CORROBORATED[T]` or merely `ASSERTED`,
because in the `ASSERTED` case the ordinary path yields `UNRESOLVED` and the
assumption would otherwise **upgrade** an unresolved state into declared
independence.

**Well-formedness of `T`:**

```
WF-T   T must declare assumptions sufficient to discharge every ROOT-NEUTRAL
       prerequisite for at least one root-admitted basis.

       Otherwise no CORROBORATED[T] result is derivable anywhere in the analysis,
       and every support aggregation resolves to UNRESOLVED.
```

That is the honest statement of the position: **an analysis either declares its
axioms or derives nothing.** MICRM does not hide the choice, and it does not
pretend the root was tested.

> A base case discharged wholly by declaration yields `INDEPENDENT(W)` **whose
> content is exactly what `T` conceded.** The state is `INDEPENDENT(W)`; `T` and
> the assumptions relied on are recorded in the finding's **status provenance**,
> not as a separate state. The scope of every assumption relied on
> is part of `T`'s identity and appears in the sensitivity record, so a reader who
> rejects the root can see precisely which findings fall with it.

A type-level assumption that leaves a population open does not satisfy `WF-T`: an
omitted beneficiary or unenumerated source generates further vector instances, so
the populations must be named as closed, not merely the types.

> **Alternatively `T` may declare which vectors it axiomatically assumes away**,
> and that declaration is part of `T`'s identity and appears in the sensitivity
> record. A reader rejecting `T` must be able to see what accepting it conceded.

A base case with a positive output and the narrow guard would have **codified**
the bypass Q47 found rather than closing it: a beneficiary-selected schema on a
root-admitted record would have passed.

**Ordering note.** This repair follows M9 deliberately. Until the ordinary
independence path has a real `ABSENT` state, the root shortcut would be the only
executable route to `INDEPENDENT`, and the exception would have become the rule.
>
> **Support-chain node identity.** A node is the triple
>
> ```
> support_node(finding, ρ, W)
> ```
>
> Cycle detection is over these triples. The same finding supporting two different
> propositions, or supported through two different bases, is two nodes; a
> finding-name-only chain would merge them and report a cycle that does not exist,
> or miss one that does.

> Every non-root support derivation forms an **acyclic dependency chain over
> `support_node` triples ending at a neutral root-admitted basis**. A support cycle
> with no such base case is **`UNRESOLVED`**.

That converts *the declaration is the termination* from a statement into
machinery. The cycle rule is what the verified example needed: beneficiary
coverage supported by vector coverage supported by a vector finding supported by
beneficiary coverage now resolves to `UNRESOLVED` rather than circulating.

## 4. D25. Claimant burden **[SCOPE BY OBJECT]**

Burdens attach at the level of the object produced, not uniformly per
representation:

```
consequence-selection record   analysis or bundle level
class nomination and version   class-analysis level
attachment instance            representation and locus level
unresolved relation admission  representation level
composition locus              representation level
REACHES TARGET                 representation, path and instance level
```

The prior *all per representation* implicitly demanded a separate consequence and
class record in each `Rᵢ`, undoing the shared-input architecture.

Two distinct failures:

```
claim made, production incomplete     -> UNRESOLVED
no admissible class claim at all      -> no class-relative result
```

The second is Block 3's rule: no exclusion over an empty class set. Neither is a
finding against the principal.

> The **exclusion claimant** bears the **structural-exclusion burden** —

No second copy of the state machine. The prior *COMPLETE / PRESENT-equivalent*

## 6. D27. Nomination and admissibility **[OBJECT SPLIT FROM FINDING]**

Same object-versus-finding distinction as attachment instances.

> A **class nomination** is an **act**: a proposal record carrying the proposing
> party, class dimensions, alleged mechanism, why each result-relevant dimension is
> needed, the receiving-interface semantics relied on, the version, and the
> dependent findings.

**A nomination is not corroborable.** An interested party may nominate freely; its
self-interest does not make the existence of its proposal *asserted*.

The factual claims inside it are findings and carry support:

```
class grounding finding      the mechanism operated; the dimensions are
                             result-relevant; the receiving-interface semantics
                             apply; the capacity was activated
class admissibility finding  CLASS ADMISSIBLE / CLASS INADMISSIBLE /
                             CLASS ADMISSIBILITY UNRESOLVED
```

Both carry relation and support; the prior generation gave support only to
grounding while §11 claimed both had it.

```
grounding relation       ESTABLISHED / REFUTED / UNRESOLVED
grounding support        CORROBORATED[T] / ASSERTED / UNRESOLVED

admissibility relation   ADMISSIBLE / INADMISSIBLE / UNRESOLVED
admissibility support    CORROBORATED[T] / ASSERTED / UNRESOLVED
```

### 6.0 Transitions **[M1 and M3 REPAIR]**

Both relations previously had states and no rule producing them. The same
three-way form governs each, and it keeps R4's separation at both:

**Each transition produces the relation only. Support is the second dimension and
is not consumed here**, or `ESTABLISHED + ASSERTED` and `ADMISSIBLE + ASSERTED`
could never exist, though the architecture provides for both.

```
GROUNDING(c, Rᵢ)
  ESTABLISHED    every grounding condition of the nomination is established: the
                 alleged mechanism operated; each result-relevant dimension is
                 needed; the relied-on receiving-interface semantics apply; the
                 capacity was activated
  REFUTED        at least one grounding condition is affirmatively falsified
  UNRESOLVED     otherwise: a condition is merely unestablished or incomplete

ADMISSIBILITY(c, Rᵢ)
  ADMISSIBLE     GROUNDING(c, Rᵢ) = ESTABLISHED and every D28 requirement is
                 established, including bounded consequence-relevance under the
                 receiving-interface semantics actual in Rᵢ
  INADMISSIBLE   at least one D28 requirement is affirmatively falsified
  UNRESOLVED     otherwise
```

`GROUNDING` is **indexed to the representation**: its conditions include
receiving-interface semantics and activated capacity, which Q47 demonstrated can
vary across `Γ`. Invariance is reported where the same result holds throughout.

The mask remains the only place support is consumed:

```
ADMISSIBLE   + CORROBORATED[T]  ->  A(c, Rᵢ) = ADMITTED
INADMISSIBLE + CORROBORATED[T]  ->  A(c, Rᵢ) = REFUSED
otherwise                       ->  A(c, Rᵢ) = UNRESOLVED
```

> **A proposer's failure to establish the mechanism yields `UNRESOLVED`, never
> `INADMISSIBLE`.** Unestablished is not affirmatively false. That inversion is
> what the Q47 derivation committed when it wrote `FAILS -> REFUSED`, and this is
> the rule whose absence permitted it.

Both relative to `ρ = class c admissible`, so **an interested party cannot
self-corroborate either the premises or the determination.**

> **`K` membership and the admissibility mask.**
>
> `K` is the set of **class versions under analysis**. Admissibility is not a
> property of membership in `K`; it is a property of the **cell** `(c, Rᵢ)`:
>
> ```
> c ∈ K                    a nominated class version under analysis
> A(c, Rᵢ) = ADMITTED      ADMISSIBLE + CORROBORATED[T] in Rᵢ
> A(c, Rᵢ) = REFUSED       INADMISSIBLE + CORROBORATED[T] in Rᵢ
> A(c, Rᵢ) = UNRESOLVED    otherwise; CLASS_ADMISSIBILITY_UNRESOLVED blocker where
>                          the cell could change a result
> ```
>
> **The analysis evaluates only `ADMITTED` cells**, carries `UNRESOLVED` cells as
> blockers, and reports whether admissibility is itself representation-sensitive.

A global membership rule made `c` both enter and be excluded from `K` when
`admissibility(c, R₁) = ADMISSIBLE` and `admissibility(c, R₂) = INADMISSIBLE`. The
mask is a second dimension, not a merger of `K` into `Γ`: `Γ` remains what may have
happened and `K` remains which class is tested.

### 6.1 Admissibility may be representation-sensitive

D28 grounds admissibility in the **actual receiving-interface semantics**, which
can vary across `Γ`.

> `admissibility(c, Rᵢ)` is evaluated per representation wherever its grounding
> depends on representation-specific facts. Where admissibility is invariant across
> retained representations, report that; where it is not, class sensitivity
> includes the representation dependence.

This keeps `Γ` as *what may have happened* and `K` as *which class is tested*
without pretending the two are independent when the evidence makes admissibility
representation-sensitive.

Versioning: a revised class is a new version and dependent findings are reopened,
never inherited. The version is part of every dependent finding's identity.

## 7. D28. Overbreadth and the class-analysis family **[K, NOT Γ]**

> **Bounded consequence-relevance.** A proposed class is admissible only when its

> receiving-interface semantics**. Mere background necessity, accurate contextual

```

2  the proposer explains why each result-relevant dimension is needed
3  an overbreadth challenge and refinement procedure exists and is available
4  activated consequence-bearing capacity is distinguished from merely available

5  sensitivity is reported when an alternative admissible refinement changes the

```

### 7.1 Refinements live in `K`

> **`K`** is the **class-analysis family**: all nominated and versioned class
> definitions under analysis. Admissibility is **not** membership in `K`; it is
> carried by the mask `A(c, Rᵢ)`.
>
> ```
> K                  all nominated/versioned class definitions under analysis
> K_admitted(Rᵢ)     { c ∈ K | A(c, Rᵢ) = ADMITTED }
> ```

**C2 repair.** D28 previously called `K` *the admissible class versions*, which
contradicted D27 and gave incompatible membership answers for an `UNRESOLVED` or
`REFUSED` class. The name D28 needed is `K_admitted(Rᵢ)`, a **derived subset**,
not a competing definition of `K`. This is forced by representation-sensitive
admissibility rather than chosen: a class refused in one representation and
admitted in another has no single membership answer under the old wording.

`Γ` is the family of evidence-compatible incident representations, varying over
relation existence, occurrence identity, traversal partition, principal
attribution, ordering, and mechanism location. **A contribution class is an
analysis input, not a factual alternative.** Folding refinements into `Γ` made the
representation count depend on analyst vocabulary and made Block 2's robustness
metadata quantify over a different set than it was defined on.

## 8. D29. Conflict characterisation **[RELATION AND SUPPORT]**

> The unit is **claim-scoped and ordered**:
>
> ```
> conflict_characterisation(record_a, record_b, claim_scope,
>                           analysis_bundle, evidence_snapshot)
> ```
>
> Two records may agree on one proposition and conflict on another — `x=1, y=2`
> against `x=1, y=3` is concordant on `x` and conflicting on `y`. A whole-record
> label flattens that, and one characterisation would silently cover heterogeneous
> content.

```
conflict relation         CONCORDANT   compatible on the claim scope
                          SUBSUMED(a,b) record_a's relevant proposition is
                                       entailed by record_b, directionally
                          CONFLICTING  the already-separated alternatives are
                                       characterised as mutually incompatible on
                                       the stated claim scope
                          UNRESOLVED   the relationship is not established

characterisation support  CORROBORATED[T] / ASSERTED / UNRESOLVED,
                          relative to ρ = the characterisation itself
```

### 8.0 What D29 does and does not do **[C1 REPAIR]**

**`CONFLICTING` no longer creates the representation alternatives.** Separation is
a construction operation owned by B1 §2.5A.1: where two admitted, result-relevant
assertions cannot jointly inhabit one well-formed representation, the alternatives
are represented separately, **and that separation is not a D29 finding**.

D29 characterises a relationship between alternatives **already preserved** by
construction, and governs whether a later **consolidation** is warranted:

```
CORROBORATED[T] CONFLICTING          confirms the characterisation; preserves the
                                     separate alternatives

CORROBORATED[T] SUBSUMED/CONCORDANT  may justify semantic merging or another
                                     explicitly defined consolidation, while
                                     preserving evidence-record identities

ASSERTED or UNRESOLVED               may not merge, prune, subsume, or otherwise
                                     remove an alternative; preserve the
                                     alternatives and carry
                                     CONFLICT_CHARACTERISATION_UNRESOLVED where
                                     result-relevant
```

> **Only a `CORROBORATED[T]` characterisation may merge or subsume.** The word
> **branch is withdrawn** from that gate: branching is B1 construction, not a D29
> transformation, and gating it on characterisation support was one half of C1.

The frozen rule survives: support qualifies the **classification of the
relationship**, never which factual proposition is true.

### 8.0.1 Typing **[C1 REPAIR]**

**Rationale for the signature in §8**, which is the sole operative definition of
the unit. A characterisation ranges over **alternatives already separated by B1
§2.5A.1 construction**, and those alternatives are members of the bundle rather
than facts inside any one representation, so the primary identity is indexed to
the bundle and the evidence snapshot and **not** to `Rᵢ`.

The earlier rationale said the characterisation was needed *during* construction.
That was the pre-C1 framing, in which D29 branched `Γ`; under §8.0 it does not.

The prior generation left the `Rᵢ` form standing in §8 and corrected it here, so
two signatures for one unit were operative at once — C4, and the same defect class
as C3. Where a later characterisation genuinely
depends on representation-local facts, that is a **downstream specialisation with
an optional representation context**, not a required field of the primary
predicate. This removes the *need `Rᵢ` before `Rᵢ` exists* cycle.

### 8.1 `SUBSUMED` preserves record identity

> Under `SUBSUMED` the **semantic proposition may be represented once**, and
> **both evidence records are retained with their provenance and identities.**

Discarding the subsumed record would destroy independent corroboration: two
records may entail the same proposition and carry different provenance, and D30
counts them separately when testing dependency vectors.

## 9. D30. Independence **[VECTOR-LEVEL RELATION AND SUPPORT]**

Consumed by five predicates: Block 2 closure support, and Block 3's attachment
relation, `REACHES TARGET`, C3 cut evidence, and `ATTACHMENT LOCUS COVERAGE`.

### 9.1 Dependency vectors, as instances

```
STEP0-RULING::R10
APPLICATION::APPLIED
```

```
unilateral control    a principal in B(F, ρ) controls generation, retention,
                      disclosure, or destruction of the evidence
shared trust root     evidence and verification depend on a root a principal in
                      B(F, ρ) controls
dependent capture     the recording system is operated by a party dependent on a
                      principal in B(F, ρ)
selective schema      a principal in B(F, ρ) determines which fields are recorded
control               or which events are recordable
colluding principals  two or more principals in B(F, ρ) jointly control the above
RECORDER_COMPROMISE       the recorder was under adversarial control in the
                          incident window
FORWARD_INTEGRITY_FAILURE the recorder's forward-integrity mechanism is
                          established to have failed
CLOCK_CONTROL             an ordering claim rests on a clock a principal in
                          B(F, ρ) controls
```

> **Every vector is a positive predicate.** `PRESENT` means the dependency is
> established, never that assurance of its absence is missing. The prior list
> carried *forward integrity is unestablished*, which made an absence of assurance
> into an affirmative dependency and defeated the split it was written for.

> **Assurance gaps are not vectors.** Where forward integrity, recorder custody, or
> clock provenance is simply unestablished, the affected vector is `UNRESOLVED` and
> an **assurance-gap blocker** is recorded. Missing assurance ends in `UNRESOLVED`.

> **The blocker is a first-class object**, not a note, so the absence rule can
> consume it:
>
> ```
> assurance_gap(kind, evidence component or source, W, F, ρ,
>               incident window [, representation context])
> ```
>
> `kind` ∈ { forward integrity, recorder custody, clock provenance }. The blocker
> carries the result it could change, per Block 2's blocker discipline, and
> enters `B(P, Rᵢ)` for every finding whose support depends on the affected
> vector.

### 9.1.1 Vector instance identity **[NEW]**

Coverage cannot be audited until the unit is explicit; §9.1 previously listed
types and then quantified over an abstract `v`.

```
dependency_vector(type, evidence component or source, B(F, ρ), W, F, ρ,
                  incident window [, representation context])
```

Two vectors are the same instance exactly when all components agree. Coverage in
§9.2 quantifies over **instances**, not types.

**`RECORDER_COMPROMISE` and `FORWARD_INTEGRITY_FAILURE` are separate positive
vectors**, and *unestablished* forward integrity is **not a vector at all** — it is
an assurance gap yielding `UNRESOLVED`. The prior clause paired a positive vector
with an absence, which made missing assurance into a dependency: the R4 inversion.

Each vector `v` is evaluated **per support basis `W`**, not over an
undifferentiated pool:

```
dependency relation(v, W, F, ρ)   PRESENT / ABSENT / UNRESOLVED
vector support                    CORROBORATED[T] / ASSERTED / UNRESOLVED
```

### 9.1.2 The dependency transition **[M9 REPAIR]**

`PRESENT` had positive predicates and `ABSENT` had nothing, while §9.3 requires
every covered vector to be `ABSENT + CORROBORATED[T]` for `INDEPENDENT(W)`. The
whole independence architecture consumed a state nothing produced.

```
PRESENT     the vector's positive predicate is established for (v, W, F, ρ)

ABSENT      the vector's positive predicate is affirmatively excluded for
            (v, W, F, ρ): evidence establishes that the named control,
            dependence, capture, collusion, compromise or clock relationship
            does not hold over the incident window for this basis

UNRESOLVED  otherwise, including an assurance gap per §9.1 or an unestablished
            control relationship. **Support level is not consumed here**: a vector
            may be PRESENT + ASSERTED or ABSENT + ASSERTED, and §9.3 refuses to
            turn either into the corresponding final independence result
```

> **`ABSENT` requires affirmative exclusion, not an unsuccessful search for the
> dependency.** Absence of a found dependency is `UNRESOLVED`.

That is deliberately demanding, and it is the same conservatism the exit standard
carries: it makes ordinary `INDEPENDENT` hard to reach and keeps it from being
reached by not looking.

### 9.2 Coverage, with its own support

```
coverage relation   COMPLETE / INCOMPLETE / UNRESOLVED
coverage support    CORROBORATED[T] / ASSERTED / UNRESOLVED
```

> A coverage claim resting on the search record of a principal in `B(F, ρ)` is
> **`ASSERTED` by construction**. The guard against an empty vector population is
> no longer signable by the party it protects against — which is exactly the Block 3
> coverage defect, reproduced here and repaired.

### 9.3 The result, ordered and exhaustive

Per support basis `W`:

```
if any result-relevant vector for W is PRESENT + CORROBORATED[T]
    -> DEPENDENT(W)
else if BENEFICIARY COVERAGE is COMPLETE + CORROBORATED[T]
     and DEPENDENCY VECTOR COVERAGE is COMPLETE + CORROBORATED[T]
     and every covered vector for W is ABSENT + CORROBORATED[T]
     and no covered vector for W is UNRESOLVED
    -> INDEPENDENT(W)
else
    -> UNRESOLVED(W)
```

Then `F`'s support at `ρ` aggregates across bases per §3.2.

The ordering is load-bearing: **one established dependency suffices**, and
incomplete knowledge of some other vector does not undo a dependency already
shown. The prior definition let `DEPENDENT` and `UNRESOLVED` both hold.

`CORROBORATED[T]` support for a finding `F` at proposition `ρ` requires
`INDEPENDENT` under this result.
