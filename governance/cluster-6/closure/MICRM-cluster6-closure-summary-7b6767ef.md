## 4. Per-candidate closure records

### 65.1 Confidentiality non-exemption
- Final state: Derivative.
- Canonical basis: v0.2 burden and unresolved-evidence rules already produce the result that restricted status does not reduce evidentiary burden.
- Surviving content: preserved guard wording, "confidentiality may constrain disclosure but cannot reduce the evidence required for an accountability claim or exit."
- Destination: guard within Restricted-evidence admissibility.

### 65.2 Claim-bounded verification
- Final state: Superseded.
- Non-derivable residue: scope-and-dependencies disclosure obligation; investigative-frame linkage on claim selection; claim-object binding.
- Corrections adopted (from the reviewer response, not the initial evaluation): independence is a property of the verification arrangement relative to the named error class, not of the evidence generator's location; claim-object binding replaces universal execution binding; Superseded replaces Derivative as the state.
- Destination: Restricted-evidence admissibility successor.

### 65.3 Execution binding
- Final state: Integration-ready core spine candidate; repaired and renamed **Runtime claim binding**.
- Canonical gap: v0.2 binds events within a run but does not link a reviewed or attested development artifact to the runtime object that participated in the selected traversal.
- Three required findings (reviewer-repaired): (1) lineage continuity; (2) claim-relative property continuity; (3) runtime participation.
- Causal ceiling (reviewer-repaired): these findings establish identity, continuity, and participation inside verified scope; they do not establish causal contribution, sole causation, correctness, completeness, or accountability attachment.
- Absorbs 65.6.

### 65.4 Manipulation-chain inclusion
- Final state: Superseded.
- Scope claim replaced by the ratified claim-bounded functional mediation boundary.
- Surviving structure: mediation-boundary eligibility (grounded, evidence-supported claim-relevant capacity on an evidence-compatible path in the relevant interval at the selected resolution), participation, dependent-claim burden. Eligibility creates an examination obligation, not ancestry.
- Observation correction: pure observation without claim-relevant effect is evidence-channel activity, not mediation.
- Destination: Mediation-boundary eligibility, participation, and coverage successor; record 66.3; conformance.

### 65.5 Classifier participation
- Final state: Superseded.
- No independent substrate-neutral core content; "classifier" is AI-substrate-specific.
- Surviving content: generalized anti-label-laundering guard (reviewer-repaired to preserve MICRM's accountable object): "A component's label, declared purpose, type, or product category does not determine its MICRM representation or establish an accountability exclusion. Represent the component according to every evidenced traversal function, evidence-channel role, and authority or accountability-path relation relevant to the selected claim." Three-dimensional representation taxonomy (traversal/Control role; evidence-channel role; authority and accountability-path relation). Classifier and cross-substrate worked examples.

### 65.6 Transformation lineage
- Final state: Superseded.
- Fully absorbed into Runtime claim binding (as finding 1, lineage continuity) and record 66.2 (per-step evidence).
- Taxonomy ruling: artifact transformations (compilation, conversion, optimization, quantization, packaging) versus runtime participation events (routing, loading, instance selection, late dependency selection); dual-role components evaluated once per evidenced function.
- Preserved guard: "An artifact transformation, packaging step, routing decision, or load event does not by itself sever upstream accountability for capacity carried through it. Severance and exit remain governed by the canonical cut and exclusion rules."

### 65.7 Alternative-verification ceiling
- Final state: Superseded.
- Single-mechanism ceiling derivable; surviving content is scope composition, seams between composed scopes, causal-explanation exclusion, and validity intervals.
- Destination: Verification scope and composition ceiling successor.

### 65.8 Record-action separation
- Final state: Derivative. (Reviewer overturned a prior correlated-evaluator recommendation of core admission.)
- Canonical basis: one machine-initiated action per traversal; one accountability record and authority chain per traversal; positive records establish only the event and outcome they record; the first invariant applies independently to each traversal. Cut condition 6 is the Gate-to-Actuation instance, not the origin of the general rule.
- Wording defect corrected: not every consequence-relevant internal transition requires a separate authority path; a distinct traversal carries its own bonded-root paths, while an internal transition requires evidenced linkage to the governing traversal and authority state; the rule is resolution-relative.
- Surviving content: Record-action exactness guard; decision-action correspondence record and conformance finding; worked cross-substrate examples.

### 65.9 Audit-boundary completeness
- Final state: Superseded.
- Sentence 2 absorbed into eligibility (and improved by grounded-capacity wording).
- Surviving element: set-level coverage, separated by the reviewer into three findings: eligible-set coverage, participation-status accounting, dependent-claim sufficiency. Positive coverage requires evidence bounding the discovery universe; failure to discover another mediator is not evidence that none exists.
- Destination: coverage clause inside the mediation-boundary successor.

### 65.10 Confidentiality cannot exculpate
- Final state: Derivative.
- Canonical basis: v0.2 already holds that unresolved evidence blocks an exit without proving wrongdoing.
- Reviewer-broadened burden clause: "Where evidence required for a claim or accountability exit is unavailable, insufficient, unbound, outside verified scope, or unresolved under direct or alternative verification, the claim or exit remains unestablished. Continued binding by burden is not a factual finding of wrongdoing."
- Destination: burden-result clause within Restricted-evidence admissibility.
