# Closure evaluation: candidate 65.4, Manipulation-chain inclusion

*Prepared 2026-07-17 (America/Denver). Evaluator: MICRM Claude (correlated rater). Source state: register r10, SHA-256 b0bb2c6023a6547fafa6143e29ddb88e5e80b19c55674f65a937588cdb0d3945, Section 65 proposition 4. Protocol v0.2 (5d9e65f8). Evaluated under the ratified claim-bounded functional mediation boundary (Sections 72 and 91), the investigative-frame authority ruling (Section 92), the unattributed state-discontinuity construction, the dual-role taxonomy ruling from the 65.6 closure, and the assigned adversarial test: an omitted or unattributed mediator must block a claim without turning latent platform capability into proven ancestry.*

## Candidate text under evaluation

"Every component that observes, labels, filters, transforms, suppresses, routes, replaces, authorizes, or determines retention of consequence-relevant state is part of the auditable run at the resolution required for the selected claim."

## Assigned question

Replaced entirely by the ratified boundary, retained as a shorter successor, or split into a core burden rule plus record and conformance requirements?

## Answer

Split. The ratified boundary replaced the candidate's scope claim but not its operational payload. What survives divides into a short core burden rule built on a three-part distinction the candidate currently collapses, a drafting-level function taxonomy with one correction, and record 66.3 as apparatus.

## The load-bearing distinction

The candidate and its sibling 65.9 sit on opposite sides of a line neither names. 65.4 includes components by what they *did*, every component that observes, labels, filters. 65.9 includes components by what they *could do*, every component capable of carrying the alleged contribution class. The assigned adversarial test lives exactly in that gap: a platform component with the capacity to have mediated, and no evidence either way. Resolving it requires separating three findings the candidate merges:

1. **Boundary membership** is determined by capacity: a component belongs to the audit boundary for a selected claim where it possesses claim-relevant mediation capacity at the chosen resolution. Membership answers what must be examined.
2. **Participation** is determined by evidence: participated, excluded, or unresolved, established from records, structural exclusion, or the capacity-exclusion test. Participation answers what happened.
3. **Burden** governs the gap: an unresolved membership question or an unresolved participation finding for a member blocks the dependent claim or exit. It establishes nothing about ancestry.

Under this triad the adversarial test passes cleanly. A retention service capable of deleting outputs is inside the boundary for a completeness-of-lineage claim by capacity. If its record channel is intervention-selective, its silence is uninterpretable under 6.1 and participation is unresolved. The completeness claim fails by burden. The service's root is not thereby found to have acted; if a discontinuity exists without attribution, the unattributed state-discontinuity record holds the fork rather than manufacturing a cause. Capability placed the component under examination; only evidence can place it in the history. This is also exactly what Section 91's guard sentence requires, now given its mechanism.

## Criterion findings

**4.1 Provenance and identity:** established; cluster 6, r8 origin, author scope ruling attached at r10.

**4.2 Gap necessity.** The scope claim, "is part of the auditable run at the resolution required for the selected claim," is Section 91 restated; replaced. The non-derivable residue is twofold: the membership, participation, burden triad above, which no existing candidate or canonical rule states and which 65.9's eventual pass will need; and the function enumeration, which is the operational definition Section 91's abstract phrase "functionally mediating" requires to be applied at all. Section 91 itself anticipated this by declining draft admission and specifying constraints for the future wording; this pass supplies that wording's structure.

**4.3 Exact-claim sufficiency, and the enumeration correction.** One member of the function list overreaches the ratified boundary: **observes**. Pure observation that reads consequence-relevant state and emits records, with no consequence-relevant effect on the run, is an evidence channel governed by the Section 6.1 machinery, not a mediator. A classifier whose label determines routing mediates; a mirror-port collector does not. The repair is conditional membership: observation places a component in the mediation boundary only where its observation has consequence-relevant effects at the claim's resolution, including where the claim itself makes the observation consequence-relevant, a timing or side-channel claim being the honest edge case that pulls an observer in. Stated this way the correction is not an exemption: the observer remains fully inside the evidence-channel frame, with emission policy, retention, and custody obligations; what it escapes is manipulation-chain membership it never earned. Remaining list members, labels, filters, transforms, suppresses, routes, replaces, authorizes, determines retention, all name consequence-relevant effects and stand, subject to the ratified dual-role rule: components are evaluated once per evidenced function, never forced into one category.

**4.4 Non-redundancy.** Against Section 91: the ruling is scope, this is mechanism; complementary, not redundant. Against 65.9: the membership clause of the successor is 65.9's capacity framing given a home, and 65.9's own pass should evaluate whether anything of it survives outside that clause; expectation recorded, not decided, per one-at-a-time. Against 65.5, classifier participation: 65.5 is an instance of the dual-role, evaluate-by-function rule and its pass should test full absorption into the taxonomy. Against record 66.3: apparatus, already correctly placed.

**4.5 Scope classification.** Core: the membership, participation, burden rule, short, on the order of the successor wordings already ratified. Drafting definition: the corrected function taxonomy under the dual-role rule. Record and conformance: 66.3, with completeness findings per claim. Naming note, drafting-level and non-blocking: the successor should align with the ratified vocabulary, mediation rather than manipulation, since the ruling's own term is functional mediation and half the enumerated functions, authorization, retention, routing, are legitimate operations; manipulation can survive as the informal alias the register already uses.

**4.6 Substrate treatment:** passes; safety instrumented systems, cloud admission controllers, email gateways, institutional registries all instantiate membership-by-capacity with participation-by-evidence without substrate wording.

**4.7 Operational findings:** membership (member, non-member, unresolved), participation (participated, excluded, unresolved), chain completeness for the claim (established, excluded, unresolved), all mapping onto 66.3 with one added membership-basis field: the capacity evidence that placed each component in the boundary.

**4.8 Evidence and dark-surface treatment:** consistent; an omitted member is manufactured dark surface, and under Section 92 the omission is owned by the claim-selection authority that scoped it out, which is the narrowing-attack answer.

**4.9 Adversarial pressure.** (1) The assigned test: passes per the triad, above. (2) Boundary flooding, an interested party inflates membership to make every claim unresolvable: bounded by claim resolution and by membership requiring claim-relevant capacity evidence, not bare possibility; the capacity-exclusion test prunes. (3) Narrowing attack, claim scoped to exclude a mediator: owned dark surface under Section 92. (4) Observer smuggling, a mediating component labeled a passive observer to escape the boundary: blocked because membership follows evidenced function, not label, the 65.5 principle generalized. (5) Ancestry inflation by investigators, treating membership as guilt: blocked by the explicit no-ancestry clause, which the successor must state, not imply.

**4.10 Interaction consistency:** consistent with Sections 91 and 92, the 6.1 record-channel machinery, the unattributed-discontinuity construction, the capacity-exclusion test, the dual-role taxonomy, and the Runtime claim binding successor, whose participation finding this triad's participation finding is the boundary-level generalization of.

**4.11 Scope-authority ownership:** the scope ruling already exists; this pass operates inside it. No further author ruling required beyond disposition.

**4.12 Open questions affected.** Cluster 6 question 12, per-claim versus universal boundary, is answered by construction: membership is claim-relative, full stop. Question 8's dynamically loaded components are members from the moment their capacity is claim-relevant, with late selection recorded as a participation event per the ratified taxonomy. The r8 construction question on candidate roots for unattributed discontinuities inherits the membership triad as its answer's frame, consistent with the capacity-exclusion direction previously offered.

## Rater note

Two elements of this pass originate with this evaluator and deserve the decorrelated look more than the rest: the observes correction, whose edge case, observation made consequence-relevant by the claim itself, I believe the conditional rule handles but which a side-channel specialist should test against timing, cache, and measurement-perturbation claims; and the triad itself, which reconciles 65.4 and 65.9 so cleanly that the cleanliness is grounds for suspicion. The specific counter-reading to test: whether membership-by-capacity quietly reintroduces the audit burden Section 72's rejected reading was worried about, unbounded examination obligations for large platforms, and whether the claim-resolution bound plus capacity-evidence requirement genuinely contains it in adversarial practice rather than only in principle.
