## Verdict

The substantive split is accepted, with one terminology repair and one burden repair.

The original 65.4 proposition is **Superseded as a standalone proposition**. Its scope claim is replaced by the ratified claim-bounded functional mediation boundary. Its surviving content moves into:

1. a core successor governing mediation-boundary eligibility, participation, and burden;
2. a corrected function taxonomy;
3. record 66.3 and conformance requirements.

`Split` describes placement, but `Superseded` is the candidate's final protocol state.

## Terminology repair: eligibility is not run membership

Capacity alone must not make a component part of the historical run.

A component enters the **candidate mediation set** for a selected claim where independently supported evidence establishes that, during the relevant interval, it possessed claim-relevant mediation capacity on at least one evidence-compatible path at the selected resolution.

That finding creates an examination obligation. It does not establish that the component acted, that its root is an ancestor, or that it belongs in the selected historical run.

The three findings should therefore be:

1. **Mediation-boundary eligibility**
   - eligible / excluded / unresolved;
   - based on evidence-supported, claim-relevant capacity on an evidence-compatible path.

2. **Participation**
   - participated / excluded / unresolved;
   - based on event, structural, or capacity-exclusion evidence.

3. **Dependent-claim burden**
   - an unresolved eligible mediator blocks only the claim or accountability exit whose validity depends on excluding or resolving that mediator;
   - it does not block unrelated claims and establishes no ancestry.

Participated components enter the selected run representation. Excluded components do not. Unresolved eligible components remain in the attribution-hypothesis set or are represented through an unattributed state discontinuity where a discontinuity is evidenced.

## Burden repair

The proposed wording that an unresolved membership question blocks the dependent claim is too broad unless `membership` is bounded.

Mere theoretical capability, unknown platform complexity, or an uninformed possibility does not create an unbounded examination obligation. The burden attaches only where there is independently supported evidence of claim-relevant capacity on an evidence-compatible path during the relevant interval.

This preserves the Section 91 guard:

- latent capability alone does not establish ancestry;
- bare possibility does not flood the boundary;
- unresolved relevant capacity can still block a completeness claim or exit.

## Observation correction

The correction to `observes` is accepted.

Pure observation that has no claim-relevant effect on state, authority, Actuation, evidence availability, timing, or retention remains an evidence-channel function, not mediation.

Observation becomes mediation where its evidenced effects are claim-relevant, including:

- measurement perturbation;
- feedback into selection or control;
- timing or side-channel effects;
- selective emission;
- retention or deletion;
- access-control effects;
- or another consequence-relevant transformation.

Dual-role components are evaluated once per evidenced function, not forced into one label.

## Recommended core successor

> **Mediation-boundary eligibility and participation:** For a selected claim or accountability exit, an identified component enters the candidate mediation set only where independently supported evidence establishes claim-relevant mediation capacity on at least one evidence-compatible path during the relevant interval and at the selected resolution. Eligibility creates an examination obligation but does not establish participation, ancestry, or contribution. Participation is separately established, excluded, or left unresolved from event and structural evidence. An unresolved eligible mediator blocks only a dependent claim or exit whose validity requires resolving or excluding that mediator; it does not establish that the component acted or that its root contributed.
