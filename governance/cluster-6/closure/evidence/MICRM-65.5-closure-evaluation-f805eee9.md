*Prepared 2026-07-17 (America/Denver). Evaluator: MICRM Claude (correlated rater). Evaluated against the ratified dual-role taxonomy (65.6 closure), the conditional-observation correction (65.4 closure), the repaired Mediation-boundary eligibility successor, and canonical v0.2's positional-Gate and functional-Control typing.*

## Candidate text under evaluation

"A classifier is placed according to its evidenced function in the selected run. It may be a Context-layer Control, Gate-stage Control, Action Selection participant, Actuation-layer Control, evidence transformer, or several of these. The label classifier does not make it external to accountability."

## Assigned question

Does anything survive beyond function-based placement, conditional observation, and the dual-role taxonomy?

## Answer

Nothing survives as core architecture. The candidate is an instance of already-ratified general rules applied to one substrate's component type. Two things survive as non-core: a guard sentence on the 65.1 pattern, and one taxonomy correction that is load-bearing for the successor's drafting and is not cosmetic.

## Criterion findings

**4.1 Provenance and identity:** established; cluster 6, r8 origin, unchanged through r10.

**4.2 Gap necessity, sentence by sentence.** Sentence 1, placement by evidenced function, is the ratified dual-role rule, components are evaluated once for each evidenced function, applied to classifiers. Absorbed; the general rule already exists and is ratified, so the instance adds nothing. Sentence 2 is an enumeration of possible placements; illustrative, and defective as written (see 4.3). Sentence 3, the label classifier does not make it external to accountability, is derivable, since accountability in v0.2 attaches by evidenced function and never by component label, but it blocks a specific and commonly attempted argument. This is precisely the 65.1 and 65.6 guard pattern: derivable, retained as preserved wording, not core. No accountability result fails if 65.5 is absent.

Worth stating for the record, because it is the pass's cleanest structural finding: **65.5 is substrate-specific in a way no other cluster 6 proposition is.** "Classifier" is an AI-substrate component type. The general rule, placement by evidenced function, is substrate-neutral and ratified. A core proposition naming one substrate's component type would violate the core's substrate neutrality on its face. 65.5's correct home is the worked-examples section, where 67.x already carries classifier pressure tests, not the proposition list.

**4.3 Exact-claim sufficiency, and the taxonomy correction.** The enumeration mixes two orthogonal axes. Context-layer Control, Gate-stage Control, Action Selection participant, and Actuation-layer Control are **stack placements**, positions in the six-layer traversal. Evidence transformer is an **evidence-channel role**, a fact about the component's relationship to the record under Section 6.1 and the cluster 1 recursion, not a position in the stack. Listing them as alternatives in one series invites the reading that being an evidence transformer is an alternative to being a control, when the two are simultaneous and independent facts. A classifier whose label gates an action and whose label is also written into the record that later evidences the run occupies both axes at once; forced to one label, it loses either its control accountability or its record-channel obligations under 6.1, emission policy, retention, custody, silence interpretation. The correction, which the drafting must carry: stack placement and evidence-channel role are orthogonal axes, and a component is placed on each independently, per evidenced function, under the ratified dual-role rule. This is the same defect class as the 65.6 operation-list correction, categories mixed into one series, and it is the second time an enumeration has smuggled a category error past its proposition.

**4.4 Non-redundancy.** Against the dual-role ruling: instance, not addition. Against the eligibility successor: a classifier's boundary eligibility follows from claim-relevant mediation capacity like any other component's; nothing classifier-specific. Against 65.8, record-action separation: 65.8 governs the distinct question of label versus the action taken because of the label, and its pass will determine its own fate; 65.5 does not overlap it, because 65.5 is about where a component sits and 65.8 is about what a downstream actor did with its output. Against canonical positional-Gate and functional-Control typing: 65.5's placements are all instances of that typing, correctly so.

**4.5 Scope classification.** Not core. Guard sentence: preserved wording, alongside the 65.1 and 65.6 guards, generalized to remove the substrate-specific noun, "a component's label, type, or product category does not determine its placement or exclude it from accountability; evidenced function does." Taxonomy correction: drafting instruction for the mediation-boundary successor and any placement text. Enumeration: worked-examples material at 67.x.

**4.6 Substrate treatment.** The generalized guard instantiates across substrates and is where the candidate's value actually lives: OT, a component labeled a monitoring relay that also inhibits; cloud, a component labeled a sidecar logger that also rewrites headers; institutional, a body labeled advisory whose sign-off is operationally required. Each is the same argument the guard blocks, and each is stronger evidence for the guard than the classifier case alone.

**4.7 Operational findings:** none new; placement findings are per-function and already covered.

**4.8 Evidence and dark-surface treatment:** the orthogonal-axes correction has direct dark-surface consequence, since a component classified only as a control has no declared record-channel emission policy, and its silence is then uninterpretable in exactly the way 6.1 forbids for exit-supporting evidence.

**4.9 Adversarial pressure.** (1) Label exemption, "it's just a classifier / just a logger / just advisory": blocked by the generalized guard. (2) Axis collapse, a component declared an evidence transformer to escape control accountability, or declared a control to escape record-channel obligations: blocked by the orthogonal-axes correction; this is the pass's live vector and the reason the correction is load-bearing. (3) Placement shopping, choosing the layer placement whose obligations are lightest: blocked because placement follows evidenced function and multiple placements coexist rather than compete.

**4.10 Interaction consistency:** consistent with the dual-role ruling, positional Gate and functional Control, Section 6.1, the cluster 1 record-channel recursion, the eligibility successor, and 65.8 (non-overlapping).

**4.11 Scope-authority ownership:** no scope expansion; no author ruling required beyond disposition.

**4.12 Open questions affected:** cluster 6 question 9, privacy-preserving records for sensitive classifier inputs, is unaffected by this disposition and remains open at the record layer where it belongs.
