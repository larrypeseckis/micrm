## Author rulings

### 1. Frozen Candidate 7.3

**Author disposition: Confirmed as a conformance or assurance claim with record.**

Frozen Candidate 7.3 does not enter the MICRM accountability core as a new invariant, core proposition, or additional root-exit condition.

Its positive claim is distinct and retained:

> A named control is non-bypassable for a selected conformance claim only where independently supported evidence establishes that every path in the bounded architecturally capable path universe must pass through that control or a qualifying alternate-path control.

An unresolved capable bypass blocks that universal conformance claim without proving that the bypass was used.

The evaluator classification survives. The evaluator's same-path-set rationale does not. Canonical MICRM-v0.2 evaluates incident ancestor paths for the selected consequence. Candidate 7.3 evaluates a bounded architectural capable-path universe. A capable route independently established as unused may defeat named-control non-bypassability while remaining absent from the incident run graph, allowing the incident accountability result to be resolved on the actual paths.

### 2. Supporting bypass-surface apparatus

**Author disposition: Confirmed as integration-ready conformance or assurance apparatus, not MICRM core operational apparatus.**

The retained apparatus includes:

- the bypass-surface record;
- explicit separation of architectural capable paths from incident ancestor paths;
- actual-bypass-use findings;
- named-control coverage findings;
- claim-relative and incident-relative dark-surface treatment;
- and the qualifying alternate-path-control test.

The apparatus may reuse evidence produced by core path, Gate, capacity, and dark-surface records. Evidentiary reuse does not make the positive non-bypassability predicate part of the accountability core.

The qualifying alternate-path-control test is claim-relative. It must establish the authority, protective scope, real refusal or modification capacity, proposal-binding treatment, validity continuity, Actuation reach, evidence support, and accountability closure required on the path it covers. Similar labels, common vendors, organizational affiliation, shared policy names, or stated purpose do not establish qualification. Qualification does not merge bonded roots or erase separate accountability paths.

### 3. Bypass-complete enforcement set

**Author disposition: Open now as a separate registered conformance candidate. Do not admit it, do not classify it as integration-ready, and do not make it a v0.3 release blocker.**

Candidate name:

> **Bypass-complete enforcement set**

Candidate object:

> A defined enforcement set is bypass-complete for a selected conformance claim only where independently supported evidence establishes that every path in the bounded architecturally capable path universe crosses at least one qualifying set member, and that the verified coverage union of the set contains every such path.

The candidate must separately resolve:

- member identity and qualification;
- whether members are alternatives, conjunctive controls, or composed controls;
- per-path member coverage;
- coverage-union completeness;
- uncovered or overlapping path classes;
- unresolved capable paths;
- investigative-frame authority over the bounded path universe;
- and the distinction between architectural coverage and incident use.

**Register state in r13:** Registered, non-blocking successor candidate.

**Closure priority:** Deferred until after the Verification scope and composition ceiling successor admission and Candidate 8.4 substrate-profile work have established the needed coverage and composition vocabulary. This defers evaluation, not registration, and does not reopen Cluster 7.
