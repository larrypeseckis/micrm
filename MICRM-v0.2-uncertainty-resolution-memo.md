This memo records the disposition of the six explicit uncertainty markers in the source review state. The complete normative wording is in `MICRM-v0.2-full-draft.md`.

## 1. Contribution-type completeness

**Prior uncertainty:** the minimum contribution fields were not safe to claim as a complete formal type system across all substrates.

**Resolution:** MICRM does not claim a closed universal type system. It defines an incident-complete alleged-contribution record. Each field is concrete, independently supported as not applicable, or unresolved. A claimed exit requires resolution only of the fields needed to distinguish capacity present from capacity excluded for that incident.

**Added machinery:** type-compatible capacity, explicit composition-locus and composition-operator fields, and five substrate examples.

## 2. Admission-traversal threshold

**Prior uncertainty:** no graph-resolution rule determined when borderline admission machinery becomes its own traversal.

**Resolution:** compare the embedded Context-boundary representation with the promoted-traversal representation. Use the embedded form only when independently supported evidence establishes accountability equivalence across bonded roots, candidate sets, paths, attachment, propagation, exits, evidence custody, dark surfaces, and selected consequence identity.

Established inequivalence plus a separately committed admission result requires promotion. If equivalence or the separate result cannot be established or excluded, retain both evidence-compatible representations in an admission-resolution fork. An exit must hold under every evidence-compatible representation in which the root is a candidate. The fork does not prove that a separate traversal occurred.

## 3. Distributed mechanisms and channel composition

**Prior uncertainty:** “structurally composable set of channels” lacked a formal relation to distributed mechanisms.

**Resolution:** a distributed contribution mechanism is a finite indexed collection of individually insufficient contribution-bearing states or capacities that become type-compatible at an identified composition locus or ordered subgraph. Indexing preserves order, repeated messages, quorum membership, and multiplicity.

The structural channel-composition test requires path continuity, incident association, temporal compatibility, actual composition semantics, mechanism-specific type composition, and consequence reach. A mechanism-specific partial operator replaces any claim of a universal composition operator.

## 4. Operative causal capacity

**Prior uncertainty:** the term lacked cross-substrate examples and risked becoming a generalized but-for test.

**Resolution:** operative causal capacity is incident-specific and path-relative. It requires presence or enablement, receiving-interface compatibility, authority and reach, quantitative and temporal compatibility, an identified mechanism or structural composition relation, and absence of prior neutralization.

Root attachment remains a separate determination. Passive carriage, background utilities, and contextual conditions do not attach a root merely because they are but-for preconditions. The draft includes examples for AI-agentic, IT/cloud, OT, cyber-physical, and institutional systems.

## 5. Nondeterministic counterfactuals

**Prior uncertainty:** no substrate-neutral standard governed stochastic or stateful controls.

**Resolution:** the full draft separates three evidence modes:

1. state-complete replay;
2. state-bounded policy-complete evaluation; and
3. matched stochastic evaluation.

Event-level difference or equivalence requires Mode 1 or Mode 2 across the complete incident-consistent control-state collection. Matched stochastic evaluation may support an overlay, assurance analysis, or population-level claim, but cannot by itself establish an event-specific MICRM exit. Mixed or insufficient results remain unresolved.

## 6. Overlay incentive effects

**Prior uncertainty:** apportionment could reward weak Gates or evidence withholding, but MICRM had no warning that preserved the core-overlay boundary.

**Resolution:** MICRM does not prescribe shares. A MICRM-attached apportionment overlay records an incentive audit covering upstream retention, evidence suppression, control avoidance, and scope minimization. The audit may state mitigations or an explicit justification for accepting an incentive. It does not change the core bound set.
